刪除帳號與資料

開發者：EvanCWChang · com.evancwchang.lumina

在 App 內刪除

使用提供帳號功能的版本，進入「我的 → 帳號 → 刪除帳號」，重新輸入目前密碼或選擇同一 Google 帳號驗證後確認，即可刪除 Lumina 的 Firebase 登入帳號。

沒有 App 也可申請

請使用註冊 Lumina 的信箱，寄信至 evan.chang.cw@gmail.com，主旨「Lumina 帳號刪除申請」，說明要刪除 Lumina 帳號。我們可能要求必要的信箱身分確認，避免他人冒名刪除；不會要求你提供密碼或驗證碼。

會刪除與會保留的資料

刪除範圍包含 Lumina 的驗證帳號、Email、UID 及儲存於該驗證帳號的基本個人資料。這不會刪除你的 Google 帳號。Firebase 說明帳號資料會在刪除後最長 180 天內從其運作中系統與備份移除；安全、防濫用或法律要求的保留依供應商政策。申請信件僅用於處理及確認該次請求。

本機日記與照片不會因刪除帳號而清除。可在 App 刪除記錄，或卸載 App 清除私人儲存空間。先前匯出到相簿、ZIP 備份或分享的副本，請至各保存位置另行刪除。卸載 App 不會刪除雲端帳號。

帳號隱私說明 · 完整政策

Delete your account and data

In Lumina Journal, open Me → Account → Delete account and reauthenticate. Without the app, email evan.chang.cw@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject “Lumina account deletion request.” We may verify email ownership; never send passwords or verification codes.

Deletion covers your Lumina Firebase authentication account, email, UID and basic profile stored with it. It does not delete your Google account. Firebase states that authentication data is removed from live and backup systems within 180 days of deletion, subject to provider security, abuse prevention and legal requirements. Request emails are used only to process and verify that request.

Your device journal is retained. Delete entries in the app or uninstall it to clear private app storage. Manage gallery exports, ZIP backups and shared copies separately. Uninstalling does not delete the cloud account.
