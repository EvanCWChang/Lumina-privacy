Lumina Journal｜拾光人生手帳 隱私權政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 28 日

本政策適用於 EvanCWChang 開發的 Lumina Journal｜拾光人生手帳（Android 套件：com.evancwchang.lumina）。

本機資料

Lumina 在手機的 App 私人儲存空間保存照片、摘要、備註、心情、標籤、記錄日期、願望及其關聯。App 不設帳號、不提供雲端同步，亦不將上述資料主動傳送到開發者伺服器。

照片匯入時會壓縮並移除 EXIF 中的附加資訊；原本的相簿照片不會被修改。App 不要求位置、聯絡人或麥克風權限。

相機與相簿

你可以主動選擇拍照或使用系統照片選擇器。拍照與照片儲存由系統功能處理；所需權限依系統版本提出。Android 匯出卡片使用 MediaStore，不要求讀取整個相簿。

備份與分享

只有在你操作匯出或分享時，資料才會交給你選擇的檔案位置或其他 App。你選擇的第三方服務適用其自身政策。ZIP 備份包含照片與文字，未額外加密，請妥善保管。

還原前會在 App 私人空間留存一份還原前備份，下次還原會取代該備份。Android 版本停用 App 自動雲端備份並排除裝置轉移資料。

本機測試統計

App 會在本機記錄啟動、日記建立與願望完成等事件的日期，用於測試期彙總。沒有遠端分析、廣告追蹤或遠端當機上報。你可自願透過系統分享匯出每日事件數；此檔不含照片、日記文字、裝置識別碼或帳號資訊。

刪除與保留

你可刪除日記或願望。刪除日記將移除目前手帳中對應的照片；既有 ZIP 備份、匯出到相簿的圖片與分享副本需另行刪除。移除 App 將移除 App 內的手帳、統計及還原前備份。你自行保存的外部檔案不會隨 App 移除。

安全

App 使用系統私人儲存空間，沒有另行實作資料庫加密。請使用手機螢幕鎖，避免未經授權的裝置存取，並保管外部備份。

年齡與用途

Lumina 面向 13 歲以上的青少年與成人，不以 13 歲以下兒童為目標。心情標記與圖表僅供個人生活回顧；本 App 不是醫療器材，不提供診斷、治療、預防疾病或專業心理健康建議。

Lumina Journal — Privacy Policy

Updated: October 28, 2026. Developer: EvanCWChang. Android package: com.evancwchang.lumina.

Data on your device

Lumina stores your photos, summaries, notes, moods, categories, dates, wishes, preferences and local usage counts in the app’s private storage. No account is required. There is no cloud sync, advertising, remote analytics or crash reporting. Lumina does not transmit this data to developer servers. Mood charts are personal reflections, not medical diagnosis, treatment, prevention or professional mental health advice. The app is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and older, not children under 13.

Photos and permissions

You choose a photo through the system picker or take one with the system camera. Imported photos are compressed locally and additional EXIF metadata is removed. Original gallery photos are not modified. The app does not request location, contacts or microphone access. Android gallery exports use MediaStore without broad gallery read access.

Backups, exports and sharing

Only your explicit export or share action sends a copy to a location or app you choose. Third-party services have their own policies. ZIP backups contain text and photos and are not additionally encrypted. Android automatic cloud backup and device transfer of app data are disabled. A pre-restore recovery backup is kept privately and replaced on the next restore. You may voluntarily export daily local test event counts; this export contains no photos, journal text, device IDs or account details.

Retention, deletion and security

You can delete entries and wishes in the app. Deleting an entry removes its photo from the current journal. Previously exported files, gallery images, shared copies and recovery backups must be managed separately. Uninstalling removes private app data and the recovery backup, but not external copies. The database and ZIP backups are not additionally encrypted. Use a device screen lock and protect backup files.

Contact and policy website

Email evan.chang.cw@gmail.com. If you contact support, your email address and message are used to answer and resolve that request. Do not send unnecessary journal entries, photos or backups.
