帳號隱私說明

適用於提供選用帳號功能的版本（0.5.0 起）；先前無帳號版本請參閱主政策。更新：2026-10-28。開發者：EvanCWChang。Android：com.evancwchang.lumina。

帳號與本機手帳

不註冊仍可使用本機手帳。Google 或 Email 帳號僅供身分識別，登入不會自動備份、同步或鎖定日記。照片、文字、心情、願望與本機統計不會因登入而上傳。共用手機上的日記不會依帳號隔離。

收集與用途

Firebase Authentication 處理 Email、帳號 ID、登入提供者、驗證狀態及密碼憑證（Email 登入時）。Google 登入可能提供姓名、Email、頭像網址及提供者識別資料。Firebase 為驗證、防止濫用與安全運作亦處理 IP 位址、使用者代理、SDK／平台版本與 App 識別等連線資訊。App 不取得你的 Google 密碼，開發者不接收明文 Email 密碼。

上述資料用於建立與維持帳號、登入驗證、安全及必要客服處理，不用於廣告或出售。App 不加入 Analytics、廣告追蹤或遠端日記分析。

服務提供者、傳輸與保留

帳號驗證使用 Google Firebase，透過加密連線傳輸。依 Firebase 公開說明，Authentication 在美國資料中心處理資料；IP 位址保留數週，其他驗證資訊保留至帳號刪除，之後於最長 180 天內從運作中系統及備份移除。服務供應商的安全、法律或防濫用保留依其政策。

刪除與控制

可在「我的 → 帳號 → 刪除帳號」重新驗證後刪除 Lumina 登入帳號，或使用公開刪除申請頁。刪除 Lumina 帳號不會刪除你的 Google 帳號，也不會清除本機手帳、相簿圖片、匯出備份或分享副本；請分別刪除。卸載 App 不等於刪除雲端帳號。

客服信件僅用於處理請求與必要身分確認，請勿提供密碼、驗證碼、私人照片或日記。聯絡：evan.chang.cw@gmail.com。

Account privacy

For versions with optional accounts (0.5.0 onward). Updated October 28, 2026. Developer: EvanCWChang. Android package: com.evancwchang.lumina.

Accounts are optional. Signing in identifies you but does not upload, sync, back up or lock your journal. Entries, photos, moods, wishes and local statistics remain on your device. Journals on a shared phone are not separated by account.

Firebase Authentication processes your email, UID, provider, verification status and password credentials for email sign-in. Google sign-in may supply a name, email, profile image URL and provider identifiers. For authentication, security and abuse prevention, Firebase also processes IP addresses, user agents, SDK/platform versions and app identifiers. We do not receive your Google password or plaintext email password. Account data is used for authentication, account maintenance, security and necessary support, not advertising or sale.

Authentication uses encrypted connections and, according to Firebase's privacy documentation, US data centers. IP addresses are retained for a few weeks. Other authentication information is retained until account deletion and removed from live and backup systems within 180 days afterward, subject to provider security and legal requirements. We do not add Analytics or upload your journal.

Delete your Lumina account in Me → Account → Delete account after reauthentication, or request deletion online. This does not delete your Google account, device journal, gallery exports or external copies. Uninstalling does not delete the cloud account. Support: evan.chang.cw@gmail.com. Never send passwords, verification codes or private journal content.
